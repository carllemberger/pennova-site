Report a bug from the app

In Pockets, open the Settings menu in the library and tap Report a Bug. It prepares an email you can read before sending. Note content is only included if you choose it.

Common questions

  • Where are my notes? On your iPad, in the Files app under On My iPad > Pockets.
  • Do I need an account? No.
  • How do I turn on AI help? It is off until you add your own Anthropic key in Settings. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you tap an AI button.
  • How do I replay the tutorial? Settings menu > Tutorial.
