Report a bug from the app

In Pennova, open the Settings menu in the library and tap Report a Bug. It prepares an email you can read before sending. Note content is only included if you choose it.

Common questions

  • Where are my notes? On your device, in the Files app under On My iPad (or On My iPhone) > Pennova.
  • Do I need an account? No.
  • How do I turn on AI help? AI is optional and for people 18 and older. Add your own key from Anthropic or OpenAI in AI settings and agree to send data to that provider. When AI is on, the text or image you use it on (and, for Auto Notes while recording, new lecture text) is sent to the provider you chose. Audio never leaves your device.
  • How do I replay the tutorial? Settings menu > Tutorial.

Your data stays yours: see the privacy policy.
