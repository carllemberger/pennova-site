Pennova – Privacy Policy (Plain Language)

The Short Version

  • All of your notes, drawings, PDFs, audio recordings and transcripts stay only on your iPad. They are not sent to us or anyone else unless you choose to share them.
  • Microphone use happens only while you are actively recording; the audio is processed on‑device and never uploaded.
  • AI features (e.g., “Explain”, “Find Gaps”) are off by default. If you turn them on you must supply your own Anthropic API key; the key is stored securely in the iOS Keychain and the request is sent only to api.anthropic.com.
  • Bug reports & diagnostics stay on your device. When you tap “Report a bug” the app prepares a file and opens Mail or the share sheet so you can see exactly what is being sent. Including note content or transcript is optional and off by default.
  • No ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking, no server‑side data collection. The developer receives nothing from the app automatically. The only things that ever leave your iPad are what you send yourself: AI requests to Anthropic (if you turn AI on) and bug reports you choose to email or share.
  • You control your data – delete notes, empty the trash, remove your AI key, or delete the app at any time.
  • If anything changes we will tell you inside the app before the new version applies.

1. What’s on Your iPad?

Pennova stores everything you create locally in its own Documents folder:

Data typeWhere it livesWho can see it?
Hand‑written notes & drawings (Ink)App’s Documents folder (iOS file protection)Only you, unless you share it yourself
Imported PDFs, Word files, imagesSame folder, visible to you in the Files app (On My iPad > Pennova)Only you
Lecture audio recordingsSame folder, protected by iOSOnly you
Transcripts (generated from recordings)Same folder, protected by iOSOnly you
AI usage logs (cost, token count)Same folder, never sent automaticallyOnly you

Because the folder is part of your iPad’s backup set, it is included in iCloud or iTunes backups if you have those enabled. (Your Anthropic key is the exception: it lives only in the Keychain, marked as this-device-only, and is never backed up or synced.) We never receive a copy of any of these files.

2. Microphone & Transcription

  • The microphone turns on only when you tap the red “Record” button.
  • Audio is recorded locally and immediately processed on‑device by Apple’s SpeechAnalyzer to produce a transcript.
  • No audio or transcript is ever uploaded to any server, including ours.

3. Optional AI Features

When are they used?

AI actions (e.g., “Explain this note”, “Ask a question”, “Find Gaps”, “Convert to text”) are only triggered when you:

  1. Turn the AI feature on in Settings, and
  2. Paste your own Anthropic API key (there is no Pennova server: the key stays in your iPad's Keychain and goes only to Anthropic).

What data is sent?

When you invoke an AI action, the app builds a single request that contains only the following:

  • A cropped image of the selected part of the page (if you chose an image‑based action).
  • A short excerpt of the surrounding transcript (a few seconds of text).
  • If you turn on Auto Notes (iPhone), the transcript text of the lecture, a few minutes at a time while you record, and again at the end for a summary. This is text only: the audio is never sent. Auto Notes is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off in the ⋯ menu at any time.
  • The note title / course / subject you typed (optional, you can leave it blank).
  • Your question or prompt.

Nothing else – no audio, no device identifier, no usage history. (Actions on a whole note, like Find Gaps or Whole Note questions, send that note's text.)

The request is sent over HTTPS directly from your iPad to api.anthropic.com. Anthropic processes the request under its own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Costs & Billing

  • You are charged directly by Anthropic on the account associated with the API key you provided.
  • Pennova stores only a local log of how many tokens you used and the estimated cost; this log never leaves the device.
  • A per‑hour spend cap is enforced in‑app (you set the maximum you are willing to spend).

Turning AI Off / Removing Your Key

  • Open Settings menu → AI Setup → “I don't want AI”.
  • To delete your API key, tap “Remove Saved Key from This iPad” in AI Setup – the key is erased from the Keychain instantly.

4. Bug Reports & Diagnostics

Pennova keeps a privacy‑safe local log that records:

  • Event names (e.g., “AI request started”)
  • Error codes, counts, durations
  • Device model, iOS version, app version, disk space, locale

Never does it record note content, transcript text, or audio.

If you encounter a crash or odd behavior, you can tap “Report a Bug” in the Settings menu. The app will:

  1. Gather the local log and, if you choose, the crash report from Apple MetricKit.
  2. Open the Mail app (or share sheet) with a preview of the files attached.
  3. Let you opt‑in to include the current note’s transcript and/or the note’s raw content (both off by default). You can see exactly what will be sent before you hit “Send”.

All data you send in a bug report goes only to whoever you send it to in Mail or the share sheet. If you send it to us at carl@lembergers.net, we use it only to fix the problem, and we delete it on request or once the problem is fixed.

5. Children & Minimum Age

The app is intended for users 13 years of age or older. If you are younger than this, please get permission from a parent or guardian before using Pennova.

7. Security

  • All files are stored in the app’s Documents folder and protected by iOS Data Protection (file encryption tied to your passcode).
  • The Anthropic API key is kept in the iOS Keychain, which is hardware‑backed and inaccessible to other apps.
  • Network traffic to Anthropic uses TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS).
  • Crash and diagnostic logs are never transmitted without your explicit action.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. When we do:

  • A notice will appear the next time you open the app.
  • The updated policy will be published at the same web address as this one.

If you continue using Pennova after the notice, it means you accept the new terms.

If you have any questions, concerns, or want to exercise any of your rights, please email us at carl@lembergers.net.
