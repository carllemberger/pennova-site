Pennova Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026 Status: Beta. Pennova is bring-your-own-key. There is no Pennova server. Who we are: Pennova is made by Carl Lemberger ("we", "us"). Contact: carl@lembergers.net.

The Short Version

  • Your notes, drawings, PDFs, audio and transcripts stay on your device. Audio never leaves your device.
  • AI features are optional and off until you set them up. You choose the AI provider (Anthropic or OpenAI, and in beta builds also Google Gemini or NVIDIA). You use your own API key.
  • When an AI feature runs, the text or image it needs is sent directly from your device to that provider's API, under that provider's terms. It does not pass through us.
  • We do not run a server for Pennova, and we receive nothing from the app automatically. No ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking.
  • The only data we receive is a bug report that you choose to email to us. You see it before it is sent.

1. What Stays on Your Device

Pennova stores your notes, drawings, imported files, recordings and transcripts in the app's own Documents folder, protected by iOS file encryption. If you use iCloud or iTunes backups, this folder can be included in those backups. We never receive a copy.

2. Microphone and Transcription

  • The microphone is on only while you are recording.
  • Speech is turned into text on your device. Audio is never sent to any AI provider or to us.

3. AI Features and Your Choice of Provider

Who receives the data

You choose one provider in AI Setup and paste your own API key for it. Requests go straight from your device to that provider:

The provider handles the request under its own terms and privacy policy. Its retention and training rules apply to the data it receives. If you pick Gemini on its free tier, Google may use the data to improve its products. Use a paid key if you do not want that.

Each provider's policy applies to what it receives. We have no agreement with these providers about your data, and we cannot see or delete data held by them. To delete data held by a provider, contact that provider.

Before any AI request, the app shows you what will be sent when that is practical. Costs are charged by the provider to your own account. You can set a spending cap in the app.

Age for AI features

AI features are for people 18 or older, because the AI providers require it. The notes app itself is for people 13 and older. If you are under 18, you can use Pennova without AI.

4. Bug Reports

  • Pennova keeps a local diagnostic log. It records event names, error codes, counts, durations, device model, iOS version, app version, disk space and language. It never records note content, transcript text or audio.
  • If you tap "Report a Bug", the app prepares the report and opens Mail or the share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you send it.
  • Note content and transcript are off by default. You can add them, and you can preview every file before you send.
  • If you email a report to carl@lembergers.net, we use it only to find and fix the problem. We keep it for up to 12 months and then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

5. Legal Basis and Your Rights

We are responsible for the bug reports you send us. Pennova does not collect personal data in the background.

  • AI features: the legal basis is your consent. You give it when you set up a provider, and you can withdraw it at any time by removing your key or turning AI off. Withdrawing consent does not affect requests already sent.
  • Bug reports: the legal basis is our legitimate interest in fixing the app, and your contract with us where a report is needed to resolve a support request. You choose whether to send a report.

You have the right to access, correct and delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict our use of it. Because almost all of Pennova's data is on your device, the simplest way to exercise these rights is to delete notes, empty Trash, or delete the app. For anything we hold (such as a bug report you emailed), write to carl@lembergers.net. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

6. Children

Pennova's notes app is for people 13 and older. Do not use Pennova if you are under 13. AI features are for people 18 and older (see section 3). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Security

  • Files are stored in the app's Documents folder with iOS Data Protection.
  • API keys are kept in the iOS Keychain with the "this device only" setting. They are not backed up or synced.
  • Requests to AI providers use HTTPS (TLS).

9. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will show a notice in the app before the change applies, and publish the new version at the same web address. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
